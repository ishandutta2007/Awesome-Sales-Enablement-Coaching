# Awesome-Sales-Enablement-Coaching

# Top Sales Enablement & Coaching Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Content Enablement, Rep Coaching & Self-Hosted Learning Platforms*
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial sales enablement and coaching platforms** and **open-source projects** that help organizations onboard reps, deliver sales content, practice pitches, and coach teams to higher quota attainment — from conversation intelligence to LMS and content management.

**Examples** include Salesforce Sales Programs, Highspot, Seismic, Showpad, Mindtickle, Brainshark, Allego, Lessonly, Gong Coaching, and SalesHood (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Sales enablement and coaching is a growing open-source domain. **Moodle** and **Canvas LMS** provide battle-tested learning platforms for onboarding and certification at scale . **Open edX** delivers enterprise-grade learning with adaptive pathways . **Frappe LMS** brings a lightweight, modern learning experience . **Formbricks** and **Documenso** handle in-app surveys and document signing for enablement workflows . **Twenty** and **EspoCRM** provide CRM foundations with activity capture for coaching. **Apache Superset** and **Metabase** power coaching dashboards. **n8n** automates content distribution and follow-up sequences. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

### Learning Management Systems

- **[Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**
  **The most widely adopted open-source LMS**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **13,000+ GitHub stars** . **Course management, assessments, certifications, and SCORM compliance** . **Used by 300,000+ organizations worldwide** . **Best for enterprise sales onboarding and certification** .

- **[Canvas LMS](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms)**
  **Open-source learning management system**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Modern interface with mobile support, analytics, and LTI integrations** . **Best for educational institutions and corporate training** .

- **[Open edX](https://github.com/openedx/edx-platform)**
  **Enterprise-grade learning platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Adaptive learning pathways, video content, and advanced analytics** . **Best for large-scale sales enablement programs** .

- **[Frappe LMS](https://github.com/frappe/lms)**
  **Lightweight, modern open-source LMS**, MIT licensed . **Course creation, quizzes, and certification tracking** . **Built on Frappe Framework** . **Best for teams already using ERPNext or Frappe** .

- **[Chamilo](https://github.com/chamilo/chamilo-lms)**
  **Open-source LMS with collaboration features**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Course management, assessments, and social learning** . **Best for European organizations** .

- **[Sakai](https://github.com/sakaiproject/sakai)**
  **Open-source LMS for higher education**, ECL-2.0 licensed . **Course management, collaboration, and portfolio tools** . **Best for academic institutions** .

### Content & Engagement Enablement

- **[Formbricks](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks)**
  **Open-source experience management platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **10,000+ GitHub stars** . **In-app surveys, link surveys, and website surveys with conditional logic** . **Privacy-first with GDPR/CCPA compliance** . **Best for capturing rep and customer feedback** .

- **[Documenso](https://github.com/documenso/documenso)**
  **Open-source DocuSign alternative**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Digital document signing with self-hosted deployment** . **Best for sales contracts and enablement materials** .

- **[Papermark](https://github.com/mfts/papermark)**
  **Open-source DocSend alternative**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Document sharing with analytics, custom domains, and access controls** . **Best for sales content distribution with engagement tracking** .

- **[Nextcloud](https://github.com/nextcloud/server)**
  **Open-source content collaboration platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **File sync, sharing, and document editing** . **Best for sales content repositories** .

- **[Outline](https://github.com/outline/outline)**
  **Open-source knowledge base**, BSL licensed . **Team wiki and documentation with search and permissions** . **Best for sales playbooks and enablement content** .

**Frameworks for building custom sales enablement and coaching solutions**: Combine **Moodle** or **Open edX** for learning management with course creation and certification tracking . Use **Formbricks** for in-app surveys and rep feedback capture . Deploy **Papermark** for sales content distribution with engagement analytics . Integrate **Twenty** or **EspoCRM** for CRM activity capture that feeds coaching insights . Use **Apache Superset** or **Metabase** for coaching dashboards and rep performance analytics . Automate with **n8n** for content distribution and follow-up sequences . Note that true enterprise sales enablement with AI-powered conversation intelligence, pitch practice role-play, and vendor-supported SLAs (Highspot, Seismic, Mindtickle) remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong learning, content, and analytics foundations that require integration for complete sales enablement operations.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Sales enablement platforms handle sensitive sales content and rep performance data. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations.
- **Content governance is critical** — sales content becomes stale quickly. Implement review cycles, expiration dates, and usage analytics to keep enablement materials current .
- **Coaching requires conversation data** — open-source stacks provide LMS and CRM foundations but lack the AI-powered conversation intelligence of commercial platforms (Gong, Chorus). Integrate call recording and transcription tools for complete coaching workflows .
- **License considerations**: Moodle uses GPL-3.0 , Canvas LMS uses AGPL-3.0 , Open edX uses AGPL-3.0 , Formbricks uses AGPL-3.0 , Papermark uses AGPL-3.0 , and Twenty uses AGPL-3.0 . Verify licensing against your use case before committing.
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong learning, content, and analytics foundations, but **AI-powered conversation intelligence, pitch practice role-play, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
